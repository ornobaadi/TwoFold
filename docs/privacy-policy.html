TwoFold privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

TwoFold is a reader and audiobook player for your own books, with a way to find free public domain books. This policy applies to the TwoFold Android app, published by Ornob Aadi.

Data we collect

None. TwoFold has no account, ads or analytics, and no server of its own. We do not receive, store or sell anything about you.

When TwoFold uses the internet

Only when you use Find free books. Then the app sends the words you search for, and the book you choose to get, straight from your phone to the site you picked: Project Gutenberg (gutenberg.org), the Internet Archive (archive.org), Wikisource (wikisource.org) and Wikimedia's book export tool (wmcloud.org), or Unglue.it (unglue.it), or a catalog whose link you added yourself (and the sites that catalog sends its files from). When you open a book's details, tap Look up details, or choose a cover, the app also sends that book's title and author to Open Library (openlibrary.org, run by the Internet Archive) to show its rating, description and covers. After you get a book from Find, it does this once by itself to keep those details and the cover with the book. Those sites can see your IP address and what you asked for, as with any website, and have their own privacy policies. TwoFold names itself ("TwoFold" and its version) in those requests and sends nothing else. Reading, listening and everything else in the app work without the internet.

Data stored on your device

Your library, copies of the book files you add, covers, reading and listening positions, saved spots and settings are stored privately on your device. Audio files stay where you keep them, and the app only reads the files and folders you choose. This data is excluded from Android device backups.

Permissions

  • Notifications: shows playback controls.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: keep audio playing while the screen is off.
  • Internet: used only by Find free books, as described above.
  • Network state: included by the audio playback library. TwoFold does not use it to send anything.

Deleting your data

Remove a book from your library to delete its stored data, or uninstall the app to delete everything. Your original files are not touched.

Children

TwoFold is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
