TwoFold privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

TwoFold is a reader and audiobook player for files you already have. This policy applies to the TwoFold Android app, published by Ornob Aadi.

Data we collect

None. TwoFold has no account, ads or analytics, and it does not have permission to use the internet. No data leaves your device through the app, and nothing is shared with or sold to anyone.

Data stored on your device

Your library, copies of the book files you add, covers, reading and listening positions, saved spots and settings are stored privately on your device. Audio files stay where you keep them, and the app only reads the files and folders you choose. This data is excluded from Android device backups.

Permissions

  • Notifications: shows playback controls.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: keep audio playing while the screen is off.
  • Network state: included by the audio playback library. TwoFold does not use it to send anything.

Deleting your data

Remove a book from your library to delete its stored data, or uninstall the app to delete everything. Your original files are not touched.

Children

TwoFold is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.
