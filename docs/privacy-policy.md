# TwoFold privacy policy

The published version is `privacy-policy.html`. Summary: TwoFold collects nothing and keeps all its data on your device. It uses the internet only for Find free books, straight to Project Gutenberg, the Internet Archive, Wikisource and Unglue.it, or to a catalog you added yourself, to Open Library for a book's rating, details and covers when you open them or choose a cover (only the title and author are sent), and to LibriVox and the Internet Archive when you look for or add a free audiobook (it then streams from and saves to your phone through Android's downloader).
