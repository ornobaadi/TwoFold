# TwoFold privacy policy

The published version is `privacy-policy.html`. Summary: TwoFold collects nothing and keeps all its data on your device. It uses the internet only for Find free books, straight to Project Gutenberg, the Internet Archive, Wikisource and Unglue.it, or to a catalog you added yourself, and to Open Library for a book's rating, details and covers when you open them or choose a cover (only the title and author are sent).
